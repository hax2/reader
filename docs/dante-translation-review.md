# Inferno translation review

Cantos VII–XXXIV were compared in full with the Italian verse in the corresponding translation blocks, including sentences that cross verse breaks. Longfellow’s wording was retained where it conveyed the source. Canto XXV required no substantive change. The entries below record changed verse lines relative to commit `e916381`; attribution-only changes are excluded.

Interpretive references consulted include [Treccani on abbicarsi](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/abbicarsi_%28Enciclopedia-Dantesca%29/) (IX.78) and [Treccani on poppa](https://www.treccani.it/enciclopedia/poppa_res-f3ea14f0-87ef-11dc-8e9d-0016357eee51_%28Enciclopedia-Dantesca%29/) (XII.97).

## Canto 07

Restore the plural travellers.

- **6:** “Shall not prevent thy going down this crag.”” → “Shall not prevent our going down this crag.””

## Canto 08

Restore the sinners’ self-regard and Dante’s fear of never returning.

- **49:** “How many are esteemed great kings up there,” → “How many hold themselves great kings up there,”
- **96:** “For never to return here I believed.” → “For I believed I never should return.”
